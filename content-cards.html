Sports Personalities

Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli (born November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India) is an Indian international cricketer and widely regarded as one of the greatest batters in the history of the sport.

Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo (born February 5, 1985, in Madeira, Portugal) is a Portuguese international footballer who plays as a forward for Italian club Juventus and captains the Portugal national team.

Max Verstappen

Max Verstappen (born September 30, 1997, in Hasselt, Belgium) is a Belgian-Dutch racing driver who currently competes in Formula One for Red Bull Racing.
